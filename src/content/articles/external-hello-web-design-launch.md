<section><p><strong>I'm so excited to announce that <em><a href="https://hellowebbooks.com/learn-design">Hello Web Design</a></em> is out today!</strong></p>
<p>I've been thinking about this book for at least two years. With <a href="https://hellowebbooks.com/learn-django">Hello Web App</a>, I taught beginner programming the way I thought it should be taught to those without programming experience. After thousands of orders and readers around the world, naturally I started thinking about <strong>how I could teach design fundamentals and shortcuts to folks without design experience</strong>.</p>
<p>These thoughts lead to a conference talk I gave at many conferences, including <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lsPrhA_m6ss">Mozilla's View Source Conference in Berlin 2016</a> and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IyHTYmSYJz8">O'Reilly's Fluent Conference 2016</a>.</p></section>
