              <section><p>It's interesting to note that while the average pledge amount was $22.48, the vast majority of backers (322 total out of 540 backers) chose the $10 pledge.</p>
<h2>Economics breakdown</h2>
<h3>Total amount deposited after fees: $10,806</h3>
<p>Kickstarter takes 5% and Amazon Payments (which Kickstarter used at the time of my campaign) takes 3-5% depending on region. </p>
<h3>Taxes: -$2,000 ($8,806 profit remaining)</h3>
<p>As soon as I got my deposit, I squirreled away $2,000 for my eventual taxes. Unfortunately shipping the books happened in the next calendar year, so I had to pay taxes on my entire payment since I couldn't claim any deductions. Note that this is a guesstimate — my taxes were combined with my husband's.</p></section>

    <img src="/static/imported/external-hwa-economics/c7fee791a5de.jpeg" alt="" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

              <section><h3>Book printing: -$1,037 ($7,769 profit remaining)</h3>
<p>I almost printed the books for ~$10 per book, which (in hindsight) would have been devastating. I ended up going with <a href="http://printninja.com/">Print Ninja</a> for the paperback printing, <strong>which ended up giving me a per-book cost of $3.60</strong> (I ordered 1,000 copies, 200 expedited, with additional costs for color pages, thicker cover and paper stock, and a spot-gloss on the cover.)</p>
<p>Kickstarter backers ended up "ordering" a total of 288 paperback books, <strong>which comes out to about $1,037 in printing costs.</strong></p>
<h3>Book shipping though Amazon: -$655 ($7,114 profit remaining)</h3>
<p>I'm selling Hello Web App through a number of different channels (<a href="http://gumroad.com/limedaring">Gumroad</a>, which has the least fees, <a href="http://amzn.to/1yLsHFH">Amazon</a>, where I get the least profit but theoretically bigger distribution, and <a href="https://leanpub.com/hellowebapp">Leanpub</a>). I read in a few sources that it was worth it to order a bunch of copies of your own book for your Kickstarter backers so you can get on Amazon's best-selling lists, which I did the day of launch.</p>
<p>I ended up ordering 55 copies of the book for various backers, which cost overall $1,159 at approximately $21 per book (the $19.95 book price plus sales tax.) I'll eventually receive money back from Amazon for the books ($8.98 per book, so $494), which brings the total cost of shipping via Amazon to $665. (There is also the cost per book for shipping to Amazon, but that ended up being around $0.30 per book so I didn't include it here.)</p>
<p>Was it worth it? I'm not sure — Amazon didn't order enough from me to cover the launch day rush (and I didn't realize at the time I could ask for an increased order) so the books went out of stock immediately. I have no way of telling, but I am <strong>pretty sure this negated the effect my book buying had on the ranking of the book.</strong></p></section>

              <section><h3>Book shipping myself domestically: -$164 ($6,950 profit remaining)</h3>
<p><a href="http://www.ebay.com/gds/Making-the-Most-of-Media-Mail-Save-Cents-on-Shipping-/10000000000902588/g.html">Did you know you can ship books domestically using USPS's "Media Mail" option for <strong>much</strong> lower shipping costs?</a> I didn't before this project. Media Mail is slower than typical shipments through USPS but the cost savings is ginormous. Shipping one book costs $2.92, and the total cost to ship books domestically $164.</p>
<p>Also — you can't print Media Mail labels from USPS.com, but you can through Paypal. However, Paypal's system is really buggy and I kept getting random errors. I also tried stamps.com, but their system seemed even worse and they required me to call them to cancel my account (and answer a survey augh), which is a terrible business practice so they're now boycotted.</p></section>

              <section><h3>Book shipping myself internationally: -$744 ($6,206 profit remaining)</h3>
<p>I made a huge mistake when I chose the additional amounts for shipping for the Kickstarter pledges. I set international shipping at $5 extra, but turns out that shipping a book internationally is much more than that — ~$8 for Canada, and $13 for overseas shipments. </p>
<p>I ended up sending a message to Kickstarter backers of the $25 tier, hat in hand, asking if people won't mind sending over the extra money to cover shipping, and a few did, some much more than I asked. The generosity of strangers always amazes me — thanks again to everyone who sent over more money.</p>
<p>The approximate total for international shipping ended up being around $844. I received around $100 in donations for shipping from various backers, which brings the total cost of shipping to $744.</p></section>

              <section><h3>Packaging supplies for shipping: -$100 ($6,106 profit remaining)</h3>
<p>Of course I needed to buy supplies for packaging my books for shipment — toner, printing labels, bags, and bubble wrap came to approximately $100.</p>
<h2>$6,000 Profit?</h2>
<p>Having ~$6,000 left on a $12,000 Kickstarter raise isn't that bad. <strong>However, the entire process took about a year, and I probably spent thousands of hours on researching, writing, editing, designing, and marketing the book before the public release.</strong> I don't actually make that much money from my <a href="http://weddinglovely.com/">day job</a> (~$20,000/year), so this profit went directly into my living expenses and <strong>I actually fell into debt the last few months because I didn't have enough money left over to pay my bills.</strong> Had I actually produced the book within the few months time period I originally estimated, $6,000 on top of producing and shipping fees isn't bad, but didn't last when stretched over a year. </p>
<p>My intent is not to complain, rather, note that while the numbers above sound great, <strong>the number of hours I spent on the project probably meant I earned less than minimum wage.</strong> Yeehah.</p>
<p><em>But</em> — the book is now selling and hopefully the debt hole I dug myself into will slowly get filled. I'm getting great reviews on the book (yay!) and have been booking <a href="https://hellowebapp.com/workshops">workshops</a> on the book's content.</p></section>
